# Cascade: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

Cascade is a home screen (launcher) app for Android. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

## The short version

- Cascade has **no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking**.
- Cascade **does not collect, sell or share** any personal data. Nothing you do in Cascade is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- Everything Cascade uses stays **on your device**.

## What Cascade uses on your device, and why

| What | Why | Leaves your phone? |
|---|---|---|
| The list of apps installed on your phone | To show and open your apps, which is what a home screen does | No |
| Notification content (title and text), **only if you turn on "Notification previews"** | To show new notifications next to the app they belong to | No. It's read only while the notification is on your phone and never stored or sent anywhere |
| How often you open each app from Cascade | In some styles, apps you use more appear larger | No, it's stored only on your phone |
| Your settings (style, theme, favorites, bookmarks) | To remember your setup | No (see "Backups" below) |

Cascade does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, SMS or call history.

## Permissions

- **Notification access** (optional, you turn it on in Android settings): shows notification previews inside Cascade. You can turn it off at any time in Android settings.
- **Vibrate**: haptic feedback for touches.
- **Expand status bar**: lets you swipe down on the home screen to open your notifications.
- **Request delete packages**: lets you start uninstalling an app from Cascade's menu. Android always asks you to confirm.

## Purchases

Cascade Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by **Google Play**. Cascade never sees your payment details. It only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can unlock Pro on your devices. Google's privacy policy applies to payments: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

## Backups

- **Settings › Backup** saves a small file with your favorites, bookmarks and style settings. It's saved **only where you choose**, and Cascade never uploads it anywhere.
- Android may include Cascade's settings in your phone's own **Android backup** to your Google account, if you have that turned on. This is handled by Android and Google, not by Cascade.

## Children

Cascade is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
